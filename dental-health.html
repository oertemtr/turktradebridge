Frequently Asked Questions

How long does dental treatment in Turkey take?

Single implants typically require 2 visits separated by 3 months (osseointegration period). All-on-4 full-arch reconstruction can be completed in 5 days with same-day teeth. Veneers and Hollywood Smile sets are completed in 5–7 days during one trip.

What brands of implants are used?

Our partner clinics use Straumann (Switzerland), Nobel Biocare (USA), MIS (Israel), and Astra Tech (Sweden) implants. All are major internationally recognised brands with verified provenance and lifetime warranty paperwork.

Are Turkish dental clinics qualified?

ExportTurk only works with clinics licensed by the Turkish Ministry of Health and the Turkish Dental Association (TDB). Many of our partner clinics hold JCI accreditation, and the prosthodontists are members of the European Prosthodontic Association (EPA) or equivalent.

What is a Hollywood Smile package?

A Hollywood Smile typically refers to 16–20 zirconia or E.max ceramic veneers covering the upper and lower visible teeth, designed to create a uniform, brighter aesthetic. The package includes consultation, scans, preparation, temporary fittings, and final placement — usually delivered in 5–7 days.

What materials are used for veneers?

E.max (lithium disilicate ceramic) is the gold standard for front teeth aesthetics. Zirconia is recommended for greater strength on premolars and molars or for full crowns. All materials are CE-certified and supplied with documentation.

Do you offer All-on-4 and All-on-6?

Yes. All-on-4 places a full arch of 12–14 teeth on 4 implants; All-on-6 uses 6 implants for higher stability. Both are completed in 5–7 days using same-day temporary teeth, with final permanent teeth fitted 3 months later on a return visit.

Will I need a translator?

Yes, an English-speaking translator is included in all packages. Arabic, Russian, German, and French translators are available on request. The clinic coordinators are usually multilingual.

How is pricing structured?

Pricing depends on the number of teeth, choice of material, brand of implant, and additional procedures (bone graft, sinus lift). Because every case is unique we do not publish ranges — email email with your dental scan or X-ray and we return a written quotation from 2–3 verified clinics within 24 hours.
