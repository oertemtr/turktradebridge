Programs Available

Türkiye has more than 200 universities, both public and private. Programs are offered in Turkish, English, and a few in Arabic or German. ExportTurk helps you find the right university, program, and funding option.

Level 01

Bachelor's Degrees

Four-year undergraduate programs across engineering, business, medicine, dentistry, pharmacy, architecture, social sciences, and humanities. Mix of Turkish-medium and English-medium options.

Duration: 4 years · Some: 5–6 years (medical, dental) · YÖS or equivalent required
Level 02

Master's & PhD

Graduate programs with thesis or coursework options. Strong English-medium offerings at private universities. PhD durations typically 4 years with one-year coursework and three years of dissertation work.

Master: 2 years · PhD: 4–5 years · Application: transcript + research proposal
Specialty 03

Medical & Dental School

Six-year MD programs and five-year DDS programs at selected universities. English-medium tracks at private universities (e.g. Koç, Bahçeşehir, Istanbul Medipol). Strong clinical exposure from year 3.

Duration: 5–6 years · Language: English / Turkish · Strict entry requirements
Foundation 04

Language Preparation

Mandatory for Turkish-medium programs. TÖMER and university-run preparatory programs deliver one year of intensive Turkish language to B2/C1 level before academic study begins.

Duration: 1 academic year · Outcome: B2 minimum · Recognised exit certificate

Your Journey, Step by Step

Most international students start the application process 6–9 months before their target enrolment semester. ExportTurk works backwards from your start date to plan the timeline.

01

Inquiry & Profile Review

You share your academic transcripts, language certificates (if any), your target field of study, and budget range. We assess your profile and within 48 hours propose 3–5 suitable universities with realistic admission outlook.

02

Application & Exams

We support your application packaging: translation of documents, apostille, statement of purpose, recommendation letters. We coordinate YÖS / SAT registration, exam preparation, and scholarship applications in parallel.

03

Admission & Visa

Once you receive admission letters from 1–3 universities, we help you select the best fit, accept the offer, and apply for your student visa at the Turkish consulate in your home country.

04

Arrival & Settlement

Airport pickup, accommodation handover (university dormitory or private), residence permit application, university enrolment day, opening a bank account, and orientation to your new city. Continuing support throughout your first year.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which universities are available?

We work with both public and private universities recognised by Türkiye's Council of Higher Education (YÖK). Public universities include leading institutions such as Boğaziçi, METU, Istanbul Technical University, and Hacettepe. Private universities include Koç, Sabancı, Bilkent, Bahçeşehir, Istanbul Aydın, and others. We propose universities based on your academic profile, target field, and budget.

Are Turkish degrees internationally recognised?

Yes. Turkish degrees from YÖK-recognised universities are accepted in 100+ countries. Medical degrees are WFME-listed and recognised by major medical councils. Engineering and business degrees from accredited universities are recognised by ABET, AACSB, and EQUIS where applicable. Document apostille is handled at the end of your studies.

Do I need to learn Turkish?

Many programs are taught in English — especially at private universities, in business, engineering, and medical schools. Programs taught in Turkish require completion of a one-year preparatory language course (TÖMER) before academic studies begin. We help you pick the language path that fits.

What is YÖS and do I need it?

YÖS (Yabancı Uyruklu Öğrenci Sınavı) is the entrance exam for foreign students applying to most Turkish public universities. Private universities accept SAT, A-Levels, IB Diploma, or run their own entrance assessment. ExportTurk supports YÖS exam registration, official preparation materials, and online mock testing.

Are scholarships available?

Yes. Türkiye Bursları (Turkish Government Scholarships) cover tuition, accommodation, monthly stipend, health insurance, and language preparation for selected international students. Many private universities also offer 25%-100% merit-based and need-based scholarships. We help with applications and timelines.

Can you help with the student visa?

Yes. Once you have a letter of acceptance from a Turkish university, we provide the document checklist for your home country's Turkish consulate, review your visa file before submission, and help with the residence permit application after you arrive.

What about accommodation?

Public universities offer government dormitories (KYK) at affordable rates. Private universities have on-campus options. For students preferring private accommodation, we can recommend reputable student housing or rental agents in the city.

How is pricing structured?

Our placement service fee depends on the scope (single university application vs full coordination across multiple universities, exam coordination, scholarship strategy). Because every student profile is different we do not publish flat rates — email email with your background and target field, and we return a written service proposal within 24 hours.
