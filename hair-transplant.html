Techniques Available

Our partner clinics in Istanbul, Ankara and Antalya use the most advanced hair restoration techniques. The right method depends on your donor area density, hair characteristics, and the design goal — we help you find the right match.

Method 01 · FUE

Follicular Unit Extraction

The standard modern technique. Individual follicular units are harvested from the donor area (typically the back of the scalp) using a precision micro-punch, then implanted one by one into the recipient site. Minimal scarring, fast recovery.

Session: 6–8 hours · Grafts: up to 5,000 · Recovery: 7–10 days
Method 02 · DHI

Direct Hair Implantation

An evolution of FUE using a Choi implanter pen. Grafts are extracted and immediately implanted without pre-opening channels — higher density placement, less time outside the body, faster healing. Ideal for smaller, denser packs.

Session: 7–9 hours · Grafts: up to 4,000 · Recovery: 5–8 days
Method 03 · Sapphire FUE

Sapphire FUE

FUE performed with sapphire blade incisions instead of steel. Sharper edges create smaller, smoother channels — denser angle control, finer hairline, less tissue trauma. Preferred for refined hairline reconstruction.

Session: 6–8 hours · Grafts: up to 5,000 · Recovery: 7–10 days
Adjunct · PRP & Beard

PRP & Beard Transplant

Platelet-Rich Plasma sessions strengthen graft survival and stimulate dormant follicles. Beard, moustache, and eyebrow transplant procedures are available standalone or combined with scalp treatment.

PRP: 30 min · Beard: 4–6 hours · Eyebrow: 2–3 hours

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a hair transplant in Turkey take?

The surgery itself takes 6–8 hours for an average 3,000–4,500 graft session. The total trip to Istanbul requires 3 days and 2 nights: consultation on day 1, surgery on day 2, post-operative wash and discharge on day 3.

What techniques do Turkish clinics use?

Our partner clinics use FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction), DHI (Direct Hair Implantation with Choi implanter pens), and Sapphire FUE (FUE with sapphire blade for finer incisions). The right technique is recommended after donor area analysis — not all patients are suitable for every method.

How many grafts can be transplanted in one session?

A typical single session ranges from 2,500 to 5,000 grafts. The maximum depends on your donor density and quality. Mega-sessions up to 6,000 grafts are possible in a single visit when the donor area supports it. Anything beyond 6,000 is usually split across two sessions 12 months apart.

Are Turkish hair transplant clinics safe?

ExportTurk only works with clinics that hold JCI accreditation, ISO 9001 quality certification, and are licensed by the Turkish Ministry of Health. Surgeons in our network are members of ISHRS (International Society of Hair Restoration Surgery) or hold equivalent international qualifications. We never refer patients to informal or non-licensed facilities.

What does an all-inclusive package include?

Typically: pre-operative consultation and blood tests, the surgery itself (FUE / DHI / Sapphire as agreed), one PRP session, a post-operative kit (shampoo, antibiotic, lotion, painkiller), 4-star hotel for 3 nights, all airport-hotel-clinic transfers, an English-speaking translator throughout, certificate of operation, and 12-month follow-up coordination. Exact contents vary by clinic and are confirmed in writing before booking.

When will I see results?

Initial growth begins at 3–4 months. By month 6, around 50–60% of grafts are visible. Full results are evaluated at the 12-month mark. We collect month 3, 6, and 12 photographs as part of our standard follow-up.

What languages do clinics support?

All ExportTurk partner clinics have English-speaking coordinators. Arabic, Russian, French, and German support is available on request — please mention your preferred language in your initial inquiry.
